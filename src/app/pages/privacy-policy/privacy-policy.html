Information We Collect

When you use Join, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: name, email address and a hashed version of your password that you provide during sign-up.
  • Content data: tasks, subtasks, contacts, board columns and any other information you create within the application.
  • Technical data: browser type, device information, IP address (anonymised) and basic log data needed to operate the service.

How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Providing the kanban functionality of Join, including creating, displaying and synchronising your boards, tasks and contacts.
  • Authenticating your sessions and protecting your account against unauthorised access.
  • Improving the application within the scope of our educational project.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Legal Basis (GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process your data based on the performance of a contract with you (Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR), our legitimate interest in operating and improving Join as an educational project (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR), or your explicit consent (Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR) where we ask for it.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on infrastructure provided by our backend service Supabase, hosted within the European Union. All connections between your browser and our servers are encrypted using TLS. Access to the database is limited to members of the student team responsible for maintaining Join. Despite our efforts, no method of transmission or storage on the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Cookies & Local Storage

Join only uses strictly necessary cookies and local storage entries that are required to keep you signed in and to preserve essential UI preferences. We do not use third-party tracking, advertising or analytics cookies.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Your data is only shared with our processor Supabase for the technical operation of the application, and where we are legally required to disclose it (for example to authorities or courts).

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access to the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure of your data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Right to restriction of processing.
  • Right to data portability in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to object to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the address below.

Data Retention

We store your account and content data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our active systems and from any backups within a reasonable period (no longer than 30 days), unless we are legally required to keep it for longer.

Children's Privacy

Join is not directed to children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can delete the information.
